8-Step Preprocessing Framework

1. Data Profiling: Profiled raw extract shape (5,075 rows × 18 columns), discovering 5.5% missing delivery dates, 3.7% missing customer segments, and 3.1% missing unit prices.
2. Duplicate Removal: Dropped 74 byte-identical duplicate rows + resolved 1 soft-duplicate pair with identical order_id by retaining the row with higher field completeness.
3. Text Standardization: Stripped whitespace and title-cased shipping_mode and customer_region, collapsing 12 dirty strings into 4 standard categories each.
4. Invalid Values: Corrected 60 negative quantities using absolute values (flagged quantity_corrected_flag); nulled 53 impossible actual_delivery_dates preceding order_date.
5. Missing Imputation: Imputed unit_price using SKU medians; mapped customer_segment to 'Unknown'; triaged missing delivery dates into In Transit (57) and imputed (222).
6. IQR Winsorization: Capped shipping_cost at $86.43 (224 outliers flagged) and order_weight_kg at 31.55 kg (285 outliers flagged).
7. Feature Engineering: Derived delivery_delay_days, is_late, order_value, and shipping_cost_log.
8. Feature Scaling: Computed Min-Max [0, 1] and Z-score (std=1) transformations for capped numeric columns.

Analytical Synthesis & Methodological Corrections (Week 4)

1. Outlier Capping Distortion: In Week 2, shipping cost was capped at $86.43 using standard IQR fences. Week 4 predictive modeling revealed that capping compressed heavy express parcels, understating the express mode premium by 80 points. Using robust absolute-error loss on the log-scale (TransformedTargetRegressor) solved this properly without truncating legitimate customer orders.
2. Systemic vs. Local Delivery Delays: Chi-square test of lateness against distribution centers yielded p = 0.899; against shipping modes p = 0.447. Delivery delays are not concentrated in a rogue DC or carrier mode, but are company-wide. Calibrating promise dates (+3 days buffer) lifts OTD from 57.4% to 92.6%.
3. Freight-Bill Auditing: Simple IQR outlier detection flagged 70 orders with 44 false alarms (precision 37.1%). The model residual detector (k = 4) achieved 100% recall with 0 false alarms, saving tens of thousands in overpayment with minimal manual review.
